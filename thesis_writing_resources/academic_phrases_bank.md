# Useful Phrases for Writing Research Papers in English

This document presents lists of frequently used phrases that have a general acceptance in all disciplines that you can use in specific sections of your paper. 

**Sections Overview:**
- Abstract: 1, 5, 2–4
- Introduction: 1–8, 9–16
- Literature review: 4, 9–16
- Methods: 17–30
- Results: 29–40
- Discussion: 35–45
- Conclusions: 45–51
- Acknowledgements: 52
- Other subsections: 53–57
