# Comprehensive Citation Styles Guide

As an academic writer, documenting all information sources is critical to credit original authors, enable readers to find your sources, and avoid the serious academic offense of plagiarism.

## General Citation Systems
In scientific publishing, three primary systems are used for in-text and end-of-document citations:

1. **Citation-Sequence**: End references are numbered sequentially as they first appear in the text.
2. **Citation-Name**: End references are listed alphabetically by author and numbered. The assigned number is used in-text whenever that source is cited.
3. **Name-Year (e.g., Harvard Style)**: In-text citations use the author's surname and publication year (e.g., `(Smith 2020)`). The reference list is alphabetical by author.

## 1. Citation-Sequence & Citation-Name Formatting
* **Journals:** `Author(s). Article title. Journal title. Date;volume(issue):location.`
  * *Example:* `Smart N, Fang ZY. A practical guide... J Card Fail. 2003;9(1):49–58.`
* **Books:** `Author(s). Title. Edition. Place of publication: publisher; date. Extent.`
  * *Example:* `Ferrozzi F, Garlaschi G, Bova D. CT of metastases. New York (NY): Springer; 2000.`
* **Book Chapter:** `Author. Chapter title. In: Editor(s). Book title. Place: Publisher; Date. p. xx-xx.`
* **Websites:** `Title of Homepage. Edition. Place: publisher; date [date updated; date accessed].`
* *Note on multiple authors:* List up to 10 authors, followed by "et al." if more than 10.

## 2. Name-Year Formatting (Harvard Style)
* **In-Text:** `(Author(s) Year)` -> `(Leboffe and Pierce 2010)`. For 3+ authors: `(Smart et al. 2003)`.
* **Journals:** `Author(s). Date. Article title. Journal title. Volume(issue):location.`
* **Books:** `Author(s). Date. Title. Edition. Place of publication: publisher.`
* **Websites:** Include author/Title, Date, URL, and [accessed date].

## 3. Chicago Manual of Style (Author-Date)
A very common standard in many disciplines, emphasizing the Author-Date format. 
* **Book In-Text:** `(Binder and Kidder 2022, 117–18)`
* **Book Reference:** `Binder, Amy J., and Jeffrey L. Kidder. 2022. The Channels of Student Activism... University of Chicago Press.` *(Note: Place of publication is no longer required in recent CMOS editions).*
* **Journal In-Text:** `(Dittmar and Schemske 2023, 480)`
* **Journal Reference:** `Dittmar, Emily L., and Douglas W. Schemske. 2023. “Article Title.” American Naturalist 202 (4): 471–85. https://doi...`
* **Website In-Text:** `(Google 2023)`
* **Website Reference:** `Google. 2023. “Privacy Policy.” Privacy & Terms. Effective November 15. https://policies.google.com/privacy.`
